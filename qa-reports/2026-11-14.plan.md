## Flows ranked by risk

### A1 — Private content read through one's own private tour + group cookie (High)

Pre: users V (victim) and X (attacker), V owns private content C (attached
to an object exhibited by museum M, so X can learn its id from
`GET /api/objects/:id?depth=0` → `contents`).
Steps: X creates a private tour T on M with stops [public c1, C] (REST POST
/api/tours) — `guardTourContents` only refuses private stops in a _public_
tour, never checks that a private stop is the tour author's. X opens a room
on T (`POST /api/rooms`), joins it (`/api/rooms/token` as guest or host) and
gets the `aa-group-tour` cookie; then `GET /api/contents/C` with that cookie.
Variant: create T with only public stops, take the cookie, then PATCH T to
add C (`groupTourContentIds` reads the tour live).
Expected: 403/404 on C, or the tour save refused. Failure: C's title/body
returned to X.

### A2 — Visit copies (`visitCopyOf`) (Medium)

- Non-admin PATCH of a visit copy with `visibility: public` and/or
  `visitCopyOf: null` → must stay private and keep `visitCopyOf`.
- POST /api/tours with `visitCopyOf` = someone else's private tour id: no
  leak of that tour (copy page, `existingVisitCopy`), copy hidden from lists.
- `/tour/new?from=<other's private tour>&visit=1`: no prefilled stops / 404.
- Concurrent "Edit" (Promise.all of 2 creates): note duplicates (Low).

### A3 — Author spoofing (Medium)

POST /api/tours and /api/contents (REST and GraphQL) as a non-admin with
`author: <other user>` → author must be the caller. PATCH with `author` →
ignored. Super admin with empty author → super admin.

### A4 — Lists and joins leaking private / personal tours (Medium)

`GET /api/users/:id?depth=1` (authoredTours, authoredContents) and
`GET /api/museums/:id?depth=1` (tours join) as anonymous and as another
user: no private tours/contents of others; no visit copies/planned visits.
`/api/navigator-search?q=…&museum=` and `/search/tours`: no private or
personal tours. Profile page `/user/:id` of someone else.

### A5 — Museum deletion cascade (Medium)

Director of M deletes M: only M's tours go. Curator of M / director of
another museum cannot delete M (and therefore its tours). Tours of other
museums by the same author untouched.

### A6 — Role escalation and tenant isolation (classic area A)

- memberships: curator grants `curator`/`director`; verified grants anything;
  user edits own row; director of M1 manages rows of M2; create a row for
  oneself.
- museums: curator updates non-map fields; staff of M1 updates M2; curator
  map save adding non-object nodes.
- objects: non-owner update; non-super-admin delete.
- contents: non-author update/delete; staff of another museum deleting.
- tours: non-author update; director of another museum deleting.
- users: read another user's email (REST, GraphQL, depth joins); update
  another user; purchases create/read others'.
- Wrong types / huge / null ids in all the above; duplicate concurrent
  requests.

### A7 — Editor routes for others' documents (Low)

`/tour/:id/edit`, `/content/:id/edit` (if any) for another user's tour,
private or public, and for a missing id: no data, 403/404 not 500.

### A8 — Printed-code redirect (`proxy.ts`) (Low)
